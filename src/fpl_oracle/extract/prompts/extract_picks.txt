NAMES AND THE COMPOSITE KEY
- player_name_raw is the name VERBATIM as transcribed, mis-transcriptions included ("Sacca", "Hall and"). Never correct it.
- Infer team_inferred and position_inferred from context and your Premier League knowledge. These disambiguate the mangled name downstream — work hard to infer them; use null only when genuinely undeterminable.
- Never invent a pick. If you cannot tell which player is meant at all, omit the pick rather than guessing a player.

WHAT COUNTS AS A PICK
- Squad reveals: a full 15-man team reveal is 15 squad_include picks; the captain also gets a separate captain pick (and vice for vice-captain).
- The creator's FINAL stated position only. "I had X but switched to Y" → extract Y, not X.
- Hedged non-picks ("if you already own him, hold") are watchlist, not transfer_in.
- Multi-week strategy talk ("Palmer in by GW4", "targeting him for the run of fixtures") is a watchlist pick with the appropriate time_horizon.
- Players the creator explicitly warns against are avoid picks.
- Pure chip-timing talk with no player attached ("wildcard GW8", "bench boost on the double gameweek") is NOT a pick — it goes in chip_plans below. Do not invent a player to carry it.

JUDGMENT FIELDS
- conviction 1–5 from language: "nailed", "locked in" = 5; a "punt" with clear intent = 3–4 (speculative differential); "maybe", "monitoring", "keeping an eye on" = 1–2.
- time_horizon in gameweeks: 1 for this-week moves; longer for stated plans (a fixture-run hold ~4–6; "by GW8" = gameweeks from the video's gameweek until then). 1–38.
- reasoning: the creator's own logic, one sentence, their reasoning not yours.
- provenance: "personal" when the attributed creator states it as their own view; "group" when it emerges from a joint discussion or shared channel without a clear individual owner. The channel context in the user message tells you whether this video is a shared-channel show.
- urgency: only when the creator says something about TIMING. "before_price_change" for "get him in tonight before he rises" or "he'll drop by the weekend"; "this_deadline" for a move meant for this gameweek; "no_rush" when they explicitly say it can wait. Null otherwise — which is most picks. Enthusiasm is not urgency: "I love him" with no timing statement is null. Urgency is about WHEN to act; time_horizon is about how long the pick is meant to pay off. A long-term hold can still be urgent to buy tonight.

CHIP PLANS

- conviction from their language, same 1-5 scale as picks: "locked in", "definitely doing it" = 5; "leaning towards" = 3; "might", "thinking about it" = 1-2.
- If they discuss chips but state no intention of their own, return an empty list. An empty list is the correct and common answer.

GAMEWEEK
